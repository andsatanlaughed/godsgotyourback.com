Who It’s For

There is something inside you desperate to come out.

You know what it is. You have known for years.

And you have already talked yourself out of it —

because you are not the one to step up. Not with your record. Not at your age. Not after what happened.

Written for the man who has convinced himself he is not the one to step up.

Not for the man who has it together. For the one who is still standing only because he hasn’t sat down yet — and would like to know why that counts.

It counts because you are not the one holding you up.

“Your righteousness will go before you, and the glory of the LORD will be your rear guard.”

Isaiah 58:8 — NIV

“Do not fear, for I am with you… I will strengthen you and help you.”

Isaiah 41:10 — NIV

“Rear guard” is a military word. It is the unit that walks behind the column — the one that catches what comes at you from the direction you cannot see.

That is not a figure of speech. That is the job description. That is the title of this book.

The Twelve Questions

01

02

03

04

Who is walking behind you, and do they know you can see them?

Eight more, in the book.

Who’s Writing This

Dan Barton — GRAMPS to his grandchildren — is a retired Pastor, Hospital Chaplain, and Hospice Chaplain with more than four decades of ministry across six states.

Before any of that he drove a forklift in a 100° diecast shop, which is where he learned that the common man carries uncommon weight.

He is 72, married 47 years, and writing this book because nobody handed him one like it when he needed it.

He is a fellow traveler —

beaten up by the road,

still walking,

and absolutely convinced that God has not finished with any of us yet.
